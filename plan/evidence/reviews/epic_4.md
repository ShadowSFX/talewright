# Review — Epic 4 (Claude bridge)

Reviewer: fresh-context subagent, 2026-10-13, on the epic 4 diff (`modules/claude_bridge`, `talewright/tools/bridge*.py`,
docs) against D-012 and the request's bridge requirements. Reported 2 high, 6 medium, 6 low findings plus test gaps.
Confirmed fine: Host/Origin strictness and check order, empty-token rejection, constant-time compare, CR/LF-stripped
response headers, lexical `res://` validation (drive letters, `..`, backslash, UNC), `frame_post_draw` deferral to the
main thread, game-side init/teardown order vs `EngineDebugger::deinitialize`, handler/log mutex ordering, every engine
API used exists. Fixes landed in task 4.13; re-verification in `plan/evidence/bridge/4.13-recheck.txt`.
